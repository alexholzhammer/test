Habit Rounds

How it works. Each round runs Monday to Sunday. Everyone picks up to 5 habits and how many days a week to do each. Tap the circle when you've done a habit today, or tap a day (M T W T F S S) to fill in another one. A “count and stay under” habit, like drinks, has a +1 button instead: tap it each time, and it earns its point on Sunday night if your week stayed at or under its max. Your score is the share of your targets you hit, and the highest score on Sunday night wins the round.
